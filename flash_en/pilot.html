Pilot: 8 weeks, 8 flashes, a public score

You get a testable probability, not an analyst's opinion: what will happen, how it reaches prices and under what conditions the forecast is cancelled.

Already fixed 10 flashes with a 90-day horizon; the first scoring is 12 Feb 2027. Until then the track record is empty by definition, and we do not hide it.

Terms

What is included8 flashes in 8 weeks: one brief per event, each with a probability, channels to assets, cancellation triggers and a check date.
CadenceA weekly digest on a fixed day and hour plus an ad-hoc flash on an event (2–4 hours after verifiable data appear).
What you receiveA one-page PDF and HTML; an asset table with current levels and thresholds; a public track record showing every forecast.
ScoringBrier and log score for the event; asset direction hits against climatology. The result is published in any case, including a miss.
Pilot priceUSD 5–15 thousand for 8 weeks (depending on the number of pairs and market-layer depth).
Subscription after the pilotSmall fund / family office — USD 2–5 thousand per month; mid-size fund or investment bank — USD 50–200 thousand per year.
What we do not doNo investment recommendations, no trading, no return promises. Asset directions are probabilistic hypotheses, not advice.
Pilot success criterionAll 8 forecasts fixed before the outcome and scored; the flash is no worse than climatology on assets; the client is ready to renew.
LimitationsMonitor probabilities are provisional (2026 labels are incomplete). Market series are Yahoo daily closes. Third-party data are not resold.

How to start

An email naming the pairs and horizon you care about. Reply within one business day; the first flash on your pair within 2–4 hours after agreement.
